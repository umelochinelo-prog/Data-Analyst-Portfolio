# Project 6: YouTube Channel Performance Analysis

**Tool:** Power BI | **Data:** 123 videos from YouTube Studio (Tales by Juliet)

![Dashboard](YouTube_Dashboard.png.png)

## Goal
Find out why some videos get many views and others don't, and what to do to grow the channel.

## Key Findings
- Full movies bring 81% of all views.
- The top 3 videos bring 69% of all views.
- Videos where 6% or more of viewers click the thumbnail get far more views.
- Videos of 20 minutes or longer get about 8 times more views than shorter ones.
- Part 2 videos get only about 40% of Part 1's views.

## What To Do Next
- Make more stories like the top 3 videos.
- Post the full story in one video instead of Part 1 and Part 2.
- Add end screens that point to another full movie.
- Post on the same day every week.

## Skills Shown
Power Query data cleaning, custom columns, KPI cards, Top N filtering, charts and turning data into recommendations.
